HOW TO HELP

Fund the step that unlocks the rest.

EqualFaith Europe’s legal work already exists. What it needs now is the legal standing to act on it at full strength — formal registration as a Dutch foundation. Every contribution, of any size, goes toward that first, concrete step.

€3,500Phase 1

The ask, plainly stated

Phase 1 covers formal incorporation, registration, and the infrastructure to operate transparently from day one. It’s deliberately small and specific, so support can be put to work in weeks, not months.

Where the money goes

The full Phase 1 budget, itemized — the same figures published on the Transparency page once funds start moving.

Budget line Amount Status
Legal incorporation (civil-law notary, statutes, deed) €800 Not yet spent
KvK (Dutch Chamber of Commerce) registration fee €85 Not yet spent
Registered office / virtual office address, NL (first 3 months) €150 Not yet spent
Public transparency website (build, hosting, domain) €1,200 budgeted Covered by founder, €1,200
ANBI application & basic bookkeeping setup €300 Not yet spent
Launch social-media campaign (creative, ads, promotion) €300 Not yet spent
Contingency €665 Reserved
The website was budgeted at €1,200 and was covered personally by the founder, not from donations — along with a further €48 for domain registration and professional email, also self-funded. See Transparency for the full breakdown. The €3,500 target itself hasn’t changed: the remaining budget lines above still need funding to complete registration.

If Phase 1 is fully funded, or exceeded

Phase 1 is capped at €3,500 by design — it’s only meant to cover registration and launch, not the full first year. If contributions exceed that amount, the surplus will be disclosed openly on the Transparency page and applied to Phase 2 core operating costs (translation, outreach, continued legal work) rather than held back or spent without explanation. Nothing above the stated budget lines gets spent quietly.
